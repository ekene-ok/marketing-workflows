# Skill: generate-angles

**Purpose:** turn a campaign brief into distinct creative angles grounded in the ICP.

## Inputs
- A campaign brief from `inputs/` (goal, audience, offer, funnel stage, promise)
- `context/icp.md`, `context/messaging.md`, `context/company.md`

## Steps
1. Read the brief and identify the single audience and funnel stage
2. Pick the one or two message pillars from `messaging.md` that fit the offer
3. Write **5 angles**. Each angle attacks a different ICP pain or motivation, in the customer's own words from `icp.md`
4. For each angle, name the proof point it can use (approved ones only), or write `[PROOF POINT NEEDED]`
5. Drop any angle that duplicates another

## Output
`angles.md`: a table with columns `#`, `Angle (one line)`, `ICP pain or motivation`, `Pillar`, `Proof point`.

## Rules
- No copy yet; angles are ideas, not ads
- Never invent statistics, customers or quotes
