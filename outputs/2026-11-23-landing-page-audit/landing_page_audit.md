# Landing page audit: Client Portal for tax season

Page: `inputs/landing_page_client_portal.md` | Ad promise: "Stop chasing clients for documents this tax season"

**Verdict: fix before sending paid traffic.** The page fails every check. It describes the whole platform to everyone, while the ad promised one thing to existing customers.

## Scorecard

| Check | Result | Threshold | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Message match (share of promise terms in headline or subheadline) | 0.17 | at least 0.5 | Fail |
| Distinct calls to action | 3 | at most 1 | Fail |
| Form fields | 9 | at most 5 | Fail |
| Reading grade level of body copy | 16.8 | at most 9 | Fail |
| Banned words | "revolutionary" | none | Fail |
| Approved proof point on page | No | yes | Fail |

## Fixes, in priority order

**1. Match the headline to the ad (message match).** Only "clients" from the ad's promise appears above the fold. Visitors who clicked "stop chasing clients for documents" land on "the all-in-one practice platform" and have to work out whether they are in the right place.
- Suggested headline: "Stop chasing clients for documents this tax season"
- Suggested subheadline: "Turn on Client Portal in your Tallyroot account. Clients upload securely, and reminders go out for you."

**2. Remove the form (form friction).** These visitors are existing customers, already signed in elsewhere, yet the page asks for 9 fields including phone, job title and current software. Replace the form with an in-account button, or cut it to work email and firm name only.

**3. Keep one call to action.** The page offers 3 different actions ("Book a demo", "Start a free trial", "Turn on Client Portal"). For an existing-customer offer, keep only "Turn on Client Portal". Demo and trial buttons are for new customers.

**4. Add the proof point.** Add: "Firms using Client Portal collect client documents 4 days faster." That is the strongest approved evidence for this exact promise.

**5. Rewrite the body copy in plain language.** At grade level 16.8, the paragraph reads like a contract. Example rewrite: "Clients upload documents to one secure place. Reminders go out automatically, so your team stops chasing. Tallyroot is SOC 2 Type II certified."

**6. Remove "revolutionary".** It breaks the brand voice rules and adds no information.

**Human review gate:** the page owner decides which fixes to make. The first three fixes are the ones most likely to lift conversion.
