# Company context: Tallyroot (fictional)

Tallyroot is a **fictional** company used to demonstrate these workflows. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

## What Tallyroot sells
Practice management software for small accounting and bookkeeping firms (5 to 50 staff) in Canada and the US.

| Product | What it does | Price (per user per month) |
|---|---|---|
| Tallyroot Core | Client records, task deadlines, time tracking, billing | $39 |
| Client Portal (add-on) | Secure document requests and e-signatures for clients | $12 |
| Smart Intake (add-on) | AI-assisted document sorting and data extraction from client uploads | $18 |
| Payments (add-on) | Online invoice payments with automatic reconciliation | 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction |

## Business model and goals this year
- About 2,400 customer firms, mostly on Core only
- Growth priority: expand existing customers into add-ons (cross-sell), especially Client Portal and Smart Intake
- Secondary priority: new customer acquisition through paid search and LinkedIn
- Tax season (January to April) is the busiest period for customers; they have the least time then and the most pain

## Channels
Paid search, LinkedIn ads, email, in-app messages, webinars, partner referrals (accounting associations).

- Firms using Client Portal collect client documents 4 days faster on average (internal study, 2025, n = 310 firms)
- 92% of support tickets answered within 4 business hours (2025 average)
