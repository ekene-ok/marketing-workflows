# Messaging framework

## Positioning statement
For small accounting firms that lose hours chasing clients and tracking deadlines by hand, Tallyroot is the practice management platform that keeps client work organised in one place, so the team gets through busy season with capacity to spare.

## Message pillars
| Pillar | Core message | Proof | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stop chasing clients | Clients upload documents through a secure portal with automatic reminders | Firms using Client Portal collect documents 4 days faster | Client Portal cross-sell, tax season |
| Never miss a deadline | Every deadline and task in one view, with owners and alerts | [PROOF POINT NEEDED] | Core, new customers |
| Get hours back | Smart Intake sorts and extracts data from client uploads | [PROOF POINT NEEDED] | Smart Intake cross-sell |
| Secure by design | SOC 2 Type II certified; client data encrypted | SOC 2 Type II | Objection handling |

## Calls to action by funnel stage
- Awareness: "See how it works" (2-minute product tour)
- Consideration: "Book a demo" or "Start a free trial"
- Existing customers (cross-sell): "Turn on Client Portal" or "Try Smart Intake free for 30 days"
