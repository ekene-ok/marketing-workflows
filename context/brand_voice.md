# Brand voice

## Voice
- **Plain-spoken:** short sentences, everyday words, no jargon our customers would not use
- **Practical:** lead with the outcome for the firm, then how
- **Calm and confident:** we reduce stress; we never add pressure

## Do
- Write to one person ("you", "your firm")
- Use the customer's language from the ICP ("chasing clients", "busy season")
- Use numbers only from the approved proof points in `company.md`
- Keep headlines under 10 words where possible

## Do not
- Use hype words: revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, best-in-class, cutting-edge, supercharge, unlock
- Use fear tactics or false urgency ("Act now or lose clients!", "Last chance!")
- Use exclamation marks in headlines
- Use em dashes or en dashes; use commas or full stops instead
- Use emojis in ads or in-app messages
