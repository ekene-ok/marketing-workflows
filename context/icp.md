# Ideal customer profile (ICP)

## Primary: Firm owner or managing partner
- Firm size: 5 to 50 staff, often 1 to 3 partners
- Owns the budget and makes the buying decision
- Biggest pains:
  - Chasing clients for documents, especially in tax season
  - Deadlines tracked in spreadsheets and email, so things slip
  - Staff time lost to manual data entry
  - Worry about client data security
- What they want: fewer late nights in tax season, more billable capacity without hiring, looking professional to clients
- Language they use: "chasing clients", "busy season", "capacity", "billable hours", "client experience"
- Objections: "We don't have time to switch tools", "Our clients won't use a portal", "Is it secure?"

## Secondary: Operations or practice manager
- Runs day-to-day workflow; influences the decision and drives adoption
- Pains: onboarding staff to processes, visibility into who is doing what, month-end and tax-season bottlenecks
- Wants: one place for tasks and deadlines, fewer status meetings, easy training

## Not a fit
- Solo practitioners (better served by lighter tools)
- Firms over 200 staff (need enterprise features we do not offer)
